## Evidence Added In v4

- Implemented deterministic local skill-survival benchmark.
- Main evaluation: 80,640 rollouts across five splits, eight methods, four skills, and seven seeds.
- Ablation evaluation: 14,112 rollouts on the combined micro-shift split.
- Stress evaluation: 201,600 rollouts over drift rate, sensor noise, mode flips, probe cost, and combined stress.
- Figures and CSVs are regenerated by `python src\run_experiment.py`.

## Main Hard-Split Evidence

- `skill_half_life_scheduler`: goal success `0.80059 +/- 0.01080`; unsafe failure `0.00099`.
- `conformal_risk_gate`: goal success `0.79811 +/- 0.01961`; unsafe failure `0.00397`.
- Paired goal-success difference versus conformal gate: `0.00248 +/- 0.02721`.
- `oracle_shift_aware_scheduler`: goal success `0.82937 +/- 0.01636`.

## Ablation Failure

The full scheduler is contradicted by a central ablation.

- Full scheduler: `0.79712 +/- 0.01168` goal success.
- Minus per-skill survival: `0.80456 +/- 0.01799` goal success.
- Minus per-skill survival also improves half-life error from `0.17594` to `0.13417`.

The proposed per-skill survival mechanism is therefore not supported.

## Submission Readiness Blockers

- No decisive baseline separation against conformal risk gating.
- A central ablation improves over the full proposed mechanism.
- Local synthetic evidence only; no real robot, high-fidelity simulator, or accepted external benchmark.

## Revival Requirements

- Evaluate on robot hardware or a recognized high-fidelity manipulation benchmark.
- Learn skill survival curves from deployment data rather than hand-designing the half-life model.
- Compare against modern uncertainty, conformal, continual-learning, and safety-gating baselines.
- Demonstrate a decisive paired gain in success or safety without hiding refresh/probe cost.
